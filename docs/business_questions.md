# Business Questions

The Gold layer is designed to answer a small set of business questions
using validated Silver data.

## 1. Revenue by completed order

How much net revenue was generated by each completed order?

Expected output: - order number - order date - net revenue

## 2. Product performance

Which products generate the most net revenue, how many units were sold, and which products deliver the highest or lowest margins?

Expected output: - product code - product name - units sold - net
revenue - total margin - margin per unit

## 3. Category performance

Which product categories generate the most net revenue?

Expected output: - category - units sold - net revenue

## 4. Store performance

Which stores generate the most net revenue?

Expected output: - store - country - number of completed orders - net
revenue

## 5. Customer performance

Which customers generate the most net revenue?

Expected output: - customer - number of completed orders - net revenue

## Gold Business Rules

-   Gold datasets are built only from validated Silver tables.
-   Revenue includes only orders with status = `completed`.
-   Gross amount = `quantity * unit_price`.
-   Discount amount = `gross_amount * discount / 100`.
-   Net revenue = `gross_amount - discount_amount`.
-   Cancelled and pending orders are excluded from revenue calculations.
-   Invalid and rejected Silver records are not used in Gold analytics.
